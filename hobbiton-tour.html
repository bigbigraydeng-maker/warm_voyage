What this day looks like

Hobbiton — the actual movie set from The Lord of the Rings and The Hobbit — sits on a working sheep farm outside Matamata, about two hours south of Auckland. You can only see it on a guided tour, and the trickiest part of the trip is the drive: rural, no direct public transport, and every big coach dumps 40 strangers into your photos.

I drive you privately in a Mercedes-Benz GLS. Pickup from wherever you're staying in Auckland, straight down State Highway 1 and 27, past dairy country, and I drop you at the Shire's Rest visitor centre in time for your booked tour slot. While you're inside the set (about 2.5 hours), I wait — no meter running. On the way back we can stop for photos, a Matamata café lunch, or the Firth Tower if you're into local history.

Common questions

Is the Hobbiton entry ticket included in NZ$1,200?

No. The NZ$1,200 covers the car and me for up to 10 hours. The Hobbiton guided tour ticket is separate — around NZ$120 per adult, less for kids, booked directly with Hobbiton. I can book the tickets on your behalf and put them on the same invoice if that's easier.

What's the earliest / latest you can pick us up?

I've done pickups as early as 05:30 (to catch the first Hobbiton tour) and returns as late as 21:00 (evening 'Banquet Tour'). Anything inside a 10-hour window is included; if the day runs over, it's NZ$100 per extra hour.

Can we combine Hobbiton with Rotorua on the same day?

Technically yes — Rotorua is another 45 minutes past Matamata — but it makes for a very long, rushed day, especially with kids. I usually recommend a 2-day plan (Hobbiton one day, Rotorua overnight the next). If you want it in one day I'll say so honestly rather than sell you a bad plan.

Child seats?

Yes — I keep two child seats (one booster, one 5-point) in the car for free. Tell me your child's age when you book and I'll check the fit.

Is it worth going if it's raining?

Yes. The set stays open in rain, and honestly the Shire looks its greenest and most cinematic when it's just drizzled. The car and Green Dragon Inn are both warm and dry, and I keep umbrellas in the boot.

Do you speak Mandarin?

Yes — native Mandarin speaker, and I've lived in Auckland for 24 years so English is fluent too. You can switch languages any time during the day.

Ready to book your Hobbiton day?

Tell me the date you're thinking, how many people, and whether you want me to book the Hobbiton tickets too. I'll come back within a few hours with a confirmed itinerary.
