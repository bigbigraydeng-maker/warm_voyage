What makes this different from the tourist ferry

Most people take the Fullers passenger ferry from Downtown Auckland to Matiatia, then either walk, catch the tourist bus, or hire a random taxi on the island. That works — but it means you spend the day queuing for the bus, waiting outside vineyards for a ride, or paying premium island-taxi rates every time you want to move.

I drive you to Half Moon Bay, put the Mercedes-Benz GLS on the Sealink car ferry, and cross with you. When we roll off at Kennedy Point on Waiheke, you already have your car and driver for the day. No queuing, no waiting, no strangers driving you between wineries when you've been tasting.

Common questions

Are you on the ferry with us, or do we meet you on the island?

On the ferry with you. I drive the car onto the Sealink car ferry at Half Moon Bay, we cross together (about 45 min), and roll off at Kennedy Point. That's the whole point — you have one car and one driver for the full day, not a passenger ferry plus a taxi at the other end.

Is the ferry ticket in the NZ$1,200?

No. The Sealink return ticket for the car (1 driver included) is around NZ$260, plus roughly NZ$45 per extra passenger. Priced by Sealink, not by me — I book it on your behalf and put the exact fare on the invoice at cost, no markup.

Can we drink at every stop?

Yes — that is exactly why people book a private driver on Waiheke. Wine tastings are usually NZ$15–30 per person, paid at the cellar door. I don't drink on the job.

Can we do this without vineyards? We don't drink.

Yes. Waiheke also has some of the best beaches within reach of Auckland, walking tracks on the eastern end, a WWII gun emplacement at Stony Batter, and a good art scene in Oneroa village. Tell me and I'll build a no-wine day.

What if the sea is rough?

The Hauraki Gulf is sheltered, so most days are smooth, and the Sealink car ferry is larger and more stable than the passenger fast ferry. If the forecast is genuinely bad the day before, I'll call and we reschedule for free.

Do you speak Mandarin?

Yes — native Mandarin speaker, 24 years in Auckland, English fluent. Switch languages any time.

Ready to book your Waiheke day?

Tell me the date, how many of you, whether you want a wine-focused day or a beaches-focused day, and any vineyards or restaurants you already have in mind. I'll come back with a confirmed plan and the ferry booking.
